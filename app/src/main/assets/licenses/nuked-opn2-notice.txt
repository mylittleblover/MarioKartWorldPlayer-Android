Included inside the replaceable libgme.so, not statically linked into PLAYER2.
Pinned upstream source and the original notices are in
third_party/game-music-emu/game-music-emu-0.6.5.tar.gz in the source repository.
PLAYER2's visible prepare-fm.cmake patch gives each instance its own initialized
chip-model state and selects YM2612 silicon for bare YM2612 VGM clock declarations.
No MAME or GENS GPL backend is compiled. The YM2413 adapter separately uses the
MIT emu2413 component; see emu2413-mit.txt.

Source and local patches:
